### Explanation
- **Date Dimension**: Represents different time frames the data can be analyzed over, such as month, quarter, and year.
- **Location Dimension**: Represents geographical locations relevant to the property.
- **Property Dimension**: Contains attributes directly related to properties, such as size and type.
- **School Dimension**: Encompasses information about the schools in proximity to the properties.
- **Category Dimension**: Describes categories properties fall into, based on various classifications.
